How the app works

Aarthika shows falling math problems or vocabulary words that a child answers by typing or speaking. Correct answers highlight and score a point.

Voice input troubleshooting

If the microphone button doesn't respond, check that Aarthika has Microphone and Speech Recognition permission under Settings → Aarthika on your device. Speech recognition runs fully on-device — see our Privacy Policy for details.

Common questions

Does this app need the internet? No — gameplay, math/word generation, and voice recognition all run entirely on the device.

Does it collect any data about my child? No. See the Privacy Policy for the full details.
