Aarthika — Privacy Policy

Effective September 20, 2026

Summary

Aarthika itself does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. There are no user accounts, no analytics, and no advertising. The only data that leaves your device at all is, on some devices, audio sent to Apple's own speech-recognition service when you use the microphone — see below.

Microphone and speech recognition

Aarthika lets a child answer questions by speaking instead of typing, using Apple's Speech framework. Aarthika always prefers on-device recognition and uses it whenever your device supports it — in that case audio never leaves the device. On devices or situations where on-device recognition isn't available, iOS falls back to sending the audio to Apple's own speech-recognition servers to produce the text, the same standard mechanism used by Siri dictation and the keyboard's dictation button elsewhere on iPhone and iPad. Aarthika itself never receives, stores, or has access to this audio in either case — only the resulting text is used, for the current question only, and nothing is ever recorded or saved.

What we don't do

  • No account creation or sign-in
  • No data sent to us, ever
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
  • No advertising or ad tracking
  • No location access
  • No data shared with or sold to anyone

Children's privacy

Aarthika is designed for children and is built specifically to avoid collecting any personal information from anyone, including children, in compliance with the intent of COPPA and Apple's App Store guidelines for apps used by kids.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the update will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
